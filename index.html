Canasta

Partnership card game

Our own house-rules Canasta, where a sharp memory wins hands. Black threes auto-meld, red threes freeze the pile, and taking the pile rewards players who've been paying attention.

  • Solo, pass-and-play, or online with friends

Dominoes

Double-six partnership

Traditional dominoes played in teams — you and your partner across the table against the other two, counterclockwise around the board, the double-six opening the first hand. First team to the target takes the match.

  • Solo vs. AI, pass-and-play, or online with friends
  • You see only your own bones — remembering is the game
  • Bones clack on wood; the winning tile slams the table

Rummikub

Runs, groups & moon jokers

Race to empty your rack by laying runs and groups — and rearranging the whole table on your turn to make your tiles fit. Our version adds four moon jokers you won't find in the box: standard, double, change-colour, and mirror.

  • Solo vs. AI with an adjustable strength dial
  • Pass-and-play, or host a table online with friends

Blokus

Corners, colours & clever fits

Claim the most squares with your 21 shapes. Every piece you add must touch your own colour corner-to-corner — and never edge-to-edge. Rotate, flip, and wedge them into the gaps before you run out of room.

  • 2-player Duo board, or up to 4 players
  • Solo vs. AI with an adjustable strength dial
  • Pass-and-play, or host a table online with friends

Why Heartside

Heartside Games is a small family studio making the kind of games you'd play around the kitchen table — the ones where the fun is in the people, not the graphics. Every game plays the same three ways: on your own, pass-and-play on one device, or online with whoever you love, wherever they are.

No accounts. No downloads. No ads. Just open a game and deal.

Only your own hand

You never see anyone else's cards or tiles — and there's no move log. Keeping track is part of the skill, the way it is at a real table.

Host from a phone

One player hosts and everyone else joins with a short code. The host's device does the work — there's no server to sign up for.

Built for family

Simple to start, easy to teach, and made to be played together — across the room or across the country.
